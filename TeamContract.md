## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We will use Discord for team communications.

* Each teammate agrees to respond to messages within a day.

* Teammates will notify each other if they think they won't be able to meet a deadline, if they miss lecture/tutorial, or if they have any issues they need help with.

* Teammates will actively listen to each team members ideas and give everyone a chance to meaningfully contribute.

---

## Decision Making

* Decisions will be made by consensus when possible.

* If consensus cannot be reached, a vote will be taken; majority rules.


---
## Conflict resolution

* Conflicts will be resolved by listening to each other's side of the issue and attempting to reach a compromise and by consulting a TA or instructor as a team, if a resolution has not yet been reached.

---

## Accountability

* All members will complete their share of the work in a timely manner, seeking assistance from teammates/TAs/instructors when required.

* All members agree to provide honest and fair peer evaluations.

---
